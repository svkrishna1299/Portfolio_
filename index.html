About

CFD engineer with an MSc in Applied Mechanics from Chalmers University of Technology, working across heat transfer, multiphase and turbulent flows.

I take a problem from geometry and mesh to validated results: DES of tyre spray behind heavy trucks with Lagrangian droplets, buoyant conjugate heat transfer of a heat sink in OpenFOAM, MRF simulations of a ventilated brake disc validated against spin-rig data, and sliding-mesh URANS with FW-H for propeller noise. Whatever repeats, I script — STAR-CCM+ Java macros, Python and SLURM on HPC clusters — and on the larger studies that went as far as neural-operator surrogates trained on hundreds of CFD runs. I now work on vehicle thermal management at Volvo Group.

Before Chalmers, I spent two years as Lead Product Engineer at Turbo Energy, developing actuators and e-recirculation valves through the full product lifecycle — hands-on product development experience that grounds how I approach simulation-driven engineering.
